# Quick Reference - Illustration Prompts & Character Design
## One-Page Guide for Fast Implementation

---

## CHARACTER QUICK SPECS

**APPEARANCE**: Stylized animated child ≠ photorealistic
- **ART STYLE**: Watercolor + digital painting (soft edges, NOT photo)
- **HEIGHT**: 3.5-4 heads tall (exaggerated proportions - intentional)
- **EYES**: Very large with sparkle highlights
- **NOSE**: Tiny simplified shape
- **MOUTH**: Curved lines expressing emotion
- **SKIN**: Warm peachy/golden tones (NO photorealism)
- **HAIR**: Simplified flowing brushstrokes

**CONSISTENCY RULE**: Same character throughout single story
- Same eye color/shape
- Same hair color/style
- Same face structure
- Same art style/technique
- Changes allowed: Expression, posture, hairstyle position

---

## FORBIDDEN (❌ NO)
- Photorealistic rendering
- Real child face replication
- Realistic skin textures/pores
- Anatomically perfect proportions
- Threatening or scary appearance
- Cool gray shadows
- Harsh black outlines

---

## REQUIRED (✅ YES)
- Watercolor or digital painting aesthetic
- Large expressive eyes with highlights
- Stylized proportions (NOT realistic)
- Warm optimistic color palette
- Soft edges and flowing lines
- Child-safe, age-appropriate
- Professional museum-grade quality
- Clear emotional expression

## LIGHTING QUICK GUIDE

**Golden Hour** (Adventure, Friendship)
- Source: Sun at 45° angle
- Direction: Warm glow illuminating character
- Shadows: Warm browns, never cool
- Color: Golds #DAA520, creams, warm highlights

**Twilight** (Mixed Adventure+Courage)
- Source: Setting sun + bioluminescence
- Direction: Diffused uplifting glow
- Shadows: Soft, warm purples
- Color: Golds transitioning to purples and teals

**Daytime** (Creativity, Kindness)
- Source: Bright sun, multiple light sources
- Direction: Flattering, inclusive lighting
- Shadows: Soft, warm undertones
- Color: Bright primaries balanced by warm harmony

**Nighttime** (Courage)
- Source: Moonlight + magical elements
- Direction: Cool moonlight + warm safety light
- Shadows: Cool dark blues, never pure black
- Color: Midnight blues #191970, purple #4B0082, warm safety golds

## FILE SPECS FOR PRINT

- **DPI**: 300 (minimum for print quality)
- **Dimensions**: 3000x3750px (for 10"x12.5" print)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 4:5 (vertical storybook)
- **Format**: PNG primary, JPG backup
- **Color Space**: sRGB (web), CMYK (print)

## COMMON REVISIONS & FIXES

| Issue | Fix | Priority |
|-------|-----|----------|
| Too photo-like | Add more watercolor, soften edges | CRITICAL |
| Cold color tones | Warm up shadows, use gold accents | HIGH |
| Scary appearance | Soften features, brighten colors | CRITICAL |
| Unclear emotions | Enlarge eyes, exaggerate expression | HIGH |
| Inconsistent style | Verify art medium match | HIGH |
| Unrealistic proportions | Review spec sheet for reference | MEDIUM |
| Over-detailed | Simplify, soften, suggest textures | MEDIUM |

---

## RED FLAGS - REJECT IF:

🚫 Looks like photograph or photo filter
🚫 Photorealistic skin, hair, or features
🚫 Threatening, scary, or inappropriate for 3-8yo
🚫 Character looks like specific real child
🚫 Cold/gray/muddy color palette
🚫 Harsh black outlines or sharp edges
🚫 Character differs from previous pages (same story)
🚫 Over-realistic anatomical proportions
🚫 Professional quality not evident
🚫 Hair/eye color changed within same story

---

## APPROVAL CRITERIA - ACCEPT IF:

✅ Clearly watercolor or digital painting style
✅ Soft edges, flowing lines
✅ Warm, optimistic color palette
✅ Stylized proportions (3.5-4 heads tall)
✅ Large expressive eyes with highlights
✅ Emotion clearly conveyed
✅ Child-safe and age-appropriate
✅ Consistent with character within story
✅ Professional museum-grade quality
✅ Perfect for 300 DPI print

## QUICK INTEGRATION STEPS

1. **Select Story Template** → Adventure/Friendship/Courage/Kindness/Creativity
2. **Choose Child Details** → Name, skin tone option, setting
3. **Get Illustration Prompt** → Use ILLUSTRATION_PROMPTS_READY.md
4. **Replace Placeholders** → {childName} → "Emma", {setting} → "the park"
5. **Send to AI or Illustrator** → With CHARACTER_DESIGN_SPEC.md reference
6. **Verify Character Consistency** → Same appearance across all pages
7. **QA Check** → Against RED FLAGS list
8. **Approve** → If meets APPROVAL CRITERIA
9. **Export Final** → 300 DPI PNGs for print

## KEY PHILOSOPHY

> Children's storybook illustrations should be **warm, welcoming, and artistically beautiful** while being **entirely fictional and safe**. The character should feel like a trusted friend—stylized, expressive, and full of emotion—never realistic or intimidating.

Every illustration should:
- ✨ Inspire wonder and emotion
- 🎨 Show professional artistic quality
- 💫 Feature stylized, fictional character
- 🌟 Use warm, optimistic colors
- 👁️ Convey clear emotional expression
- 🛡️ Be completely child-safe
- 📖 Support the story narrative
