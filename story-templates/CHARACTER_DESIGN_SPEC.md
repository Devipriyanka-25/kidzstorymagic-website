# Character Design Specification Sheet
## Technical Reference for Illustrators & AI Art Services

---

## CHARACTER PROFILE

### Identity
- **Name**: Variable (personalized with child's name)
- **Age**: 3-8 years old (illustrated as stylized cartoon, not realistic)
- **Story Role**: Main protagonist across all pages within a single story
- **Design Style**: Watercolor-digital painting hybrid, stylized animation

### CRITICAL DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

⚠️ **MUST INCLUDE:**
- ✅ Stylized, animated appearance (NOT photorealistic)
- ✅ Exaggerated, expressive features capturing emotion
- ✅ Consistent appearance across all pages within same story
- ✅ Watercolor or digital painting rendering (soft edges)
- ✅ Child-friendly, non-threatening design
- ✅ Warm, optimistic color palette

❌ **ABSOLUTELY PROHIBITED:**
- ❌ NO photorealistic rendering
- ❌ NO replication of real child's facial features
- ❌ NO detailed realistic skin texture
- ❌ NO specific identifying characteristics
- ❌ NO scary or threatening elements
- ❌ NO anatomically precise proportions

## PHYSICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### Height & Proportion
```
TOTAL HEIGHT PROPORTION: 3.5-4 heads tall (stylized)

Part Ratios:
├─ Head: 1/3 to 1/4 of total body height
├─ Torso: Slightly shorter than realistic
├─ Arms: Proportional to child, simplified
├─ Legs: Child-appropriate length
└─ Overall: Soft, rounded, never angular or threatening

ANATOMY NOTE: NOT realistic human proportions
These exaggerated proportions are intentional and necessary for 
stylized children's book illustration aesthetic
```

### Head & Face Specifications

**HEAD SHAPE**
```
Shape: Slightly rounded/circular
Size: Disproportionately large (1/4 of body)
Style: Gentle rounded not angular
Rendering: Soft watercolor edges
```

**EYES** (Most Important Feature)
```
Size: Large relative to face (takes up ~1/3 of face width)
Position: Slightly separated, placed in upper-middle of face
Shape: Almond or circular, rounded
Highlights: 2-3 sparkle highlights showing emotion and wonder
Lashes: Long, expressive (not realistic detail)
Color: Variable - hazel, brown, blue, green, etc. (warm tone)
Expression Conveyance: Eyes should show primary emotion
Rendering: Soft with defined highlights, not photorealistic
```

**NOSE**
```
Style: Simplified geometric shape
Options: Small triangle, dot, or minimal curve
Size: Very small relative to face (roughly 1/6 of eye width)
Color: Slightly different shade from skin (pink or brown tint)
Positioning: Centered, in upper-middle of face
Rendering: Minimal details, soft edges
Purpose: Suggests facial structure without realistic detail
```

**MOUTH**
```
Style: Curved lines suggesting expression
Options: 
- Happy/excited: ⌣ curved smile to eye level
- Surprised: O shape (not gaping)
- Thoughtful: — straight or slightly curved down
- Determined: — straight with subtle tension

Color: Warm tones (pink, peachy tones, not realistic lip detail)
Position: Lower third of face
Rendering: Curved lines, rarely perfectly realistic
Teeth: Show only if smiling (simplified, not detailed)
```

**HAIR**
```
Style: Simplified, flowing, expressive
NOT detailed realistic strands but suggested texture
Rendering: Watercolor brushstrokes suggesting volume
Length: Variable (short, medium, long all acceptable)
Color: Variable options available
Texture: Painted texture, suggests softness

Examples:
- Straight flowing
- Wavy with movement
- Curly textured appearance
- Can show hair type diversity while maintaining stylization
```

**EARS**
```
Size: Small, simplified
Style: Geometric shape or small curve
Position: Sides of head
Detail: Minimal - not realistic ear anatomy
Visibility: Often partially hidden by hair
```

### Body Specifications

**TORSO/CHEST**
```
Shape: Soft rounded, not angular
Size: Slightly smaller than realistic proportions
Clothing: Child-appropriate, age-suitable
Posture: Can show emotion through positioning
Proportions: Narrower than adult, rounder than realistic child
```

**ARMS & HANDS**
```
Arms:
- Length: Child-proportional, slightly exaggerated
- Shape: Rounded, soft
- Positioning: Expressive, shows emotion/action

Hands:
- Style: Simplified, few fingers shown
- Detail: Minimal, no realistic wrinkles/veins
- Positioning: Expressive positioning shows emotion
- Options: May show 3-4 fingers or mittens-style simplified

Usage: Important for conveying action and emotion
```

**LEGS & FEET**
```
Legs:
- Proportions: Child-appropriate length
- Shape: Slightly tapered, soft
- Color: Skin tone where visible

Feet:
- Style: Simplified, minimal detail
- Size: Proportional to legs
- Shoes: Often wear child-appropriate footwear
```

### Skin Tone Specifications

**DEFAULT PALETTE** (Warm Tones - Primary Option)
```
Option 1: Peachy-Cream
- RGB: (255, 200, 170) or similar warm peachy range
- Hex: #FFC8AA
- LCh: Light, warm, slightly saturated

Option 2: Golden-Warm
- RGB: (255, 215, 175)
- Hex: #FFD7AF
- LCh: Slightly more saturated warmth

Option 3: Cream-Beige
- RGB: (245, 230, 211)
- Hex: #F5E6D3
- For lighter skin representation
```

**DIVERSITY PALETTE** (Maintaining Illustration Style)
```
All warm-toned, illustrated (NOT photorealistic):

Warm Brown:
- RGB: (210, 160, 120)
- Hex: #D2A078
- Warm-toned brown maintaining illustration style

Medium Brown:
- RGB: (180, 130, 90)
- Hex: #B4825A
- Rich warm brown tone

Deep Warm:
- RGB: (160, 110, 70)
- Hex: #A06E46
- Darker warm tone

Golden Brown:
- RGB: (200, 150, 100)
- Hex: #C89664
- Warm golden undertone brown
```

**COLOR RENDERING REQUIREMENTS**
- ✅ Always maintain illustrated/painted appearance
- ✅ All tones should be warm (never cool/gray undertones)
- ✅ No photorealistic skin texture
- ✅ No pore detail or realistic skin patterns
- ✅ Soft watercolor edges, not defined boundaries
- ❌ Never look like photograph correction
- ❌ Never use pure white highlights (slightly warm)

## EXPRESSION & EMOTION RENDERING

### Emotional States by Story

#### HAPPINESS / JOY
```
Eyes: Large and sparkling with extra highlights
      Upper lids raised, full whites visible
      Eyebrows: Raised slightly
Mouth: Full curved smile reaching eyes
       Can show subtle teeth
       Sides of mouth high
Cheeks: Warm rosy glow (peachy wash)
Body: Relaxed, open posture
Overall: Warm, light feeling
```

#### EXCITEMENT / WONDER
```
Eyes: Maximum size, very wide
      Multiple sparkle highlights
      Looking toward object of wonder
Mouth: Open smile or O shape
       Eyebrows: Raised
Cheeks: Rosy, healthy glow
Body: Dynamic posture, engaged
Hands: Reaching or pointing toward wonder
Overall: Energy and amazement
```

#### DETERMINATION / COURAGE
```
Eyes: Focused forward, direct gaze
      Eyebrows: Slightly narrowed or straight
Mouth: Neutral or slight smile
       Lips together, showing resolve
Jaw: Subtle tension suggested
Body: Straight posture, standing tall
Hands: Ready position or purposeful gesture
Overall: Inner strength and resolve
```

#### CONCERN / WORRY
```
Eyes: Slightly larger than normal
      Eyebrows: Angled down-inward (V shape)
Mouth: Slight downturn
       Sometimes neutral, not fully frowning
Cheeks: Less vibrant than happy
Forehead: Slight crease/tension suggested
Body: Slight tension in posture
Overall: Age-appropriate concern (not frightening)
```

#### SADNESS / CONCERN FOR OTHERS
```
Eyes: Soft and rounded
      Eyebrows: Angled down-inward
      Possible tears (gentle, not graphic)
Mouth: Slight downturn
       Considering/thoughtful expression
Cheeks: Warm but less vibrant
Body: Slightly withdrawn but not defeated
Overall: Emotional depth, genuine feeling
```

#### THOUGHTFULNESS / CONCENTRATION
```
Eyes: Slightly narrowed but soft
      Eyes directed toward thought subject
      Eyebrows: Slightly raised or neutral
Mouth: Neutral or slight smile
       Sometimes hand to chin
Cheeks: Natural tone, warm
Body: Engaged but calm posture
Overall: Focused, considering, intelligent
```

## CONSISTENCY GUIDELINES - WITHIN STORY

### Same Story - Character Must Remain Consistent

1. **Eye Shape**: Same eye shape throughout story
2. **Eye Color**: Same eye color (unless lighting changes naturally)
3. **Hair Style**: Same hair style (may flow differently with movement)
4. **Hair Color**: Same hair color
5. **Skin Tone**: Same skin tone (lighting may affect appearance)
6. **Facial Structure**: Same face shape, proportions
7. **Style**: Same watercolor style, rendering technique
8. **Character**: Same personality shown through expressions

### What CAN Change
✓ Expression (reflects emotion of scene)
✓ Hairstyle positioning (flowing with movement)
✓ Clothing (but usually consistent within single story)
✓ Posture (reflects action and emotion)
✓ Apparent size (perspective changes)

### What MUST NOT Change
✗ Fundamental face structure
✗ Eye color or shape
✗ Hair color or fundamental style
✗ Rendering technique/style
✗ Artistic quality level

## ARTISTIC STYLE - RENDERING TECHNIQUE

### Recommended Medium Combination
```
PRIMARY: Watercolor
- Soft edges
- Color transparency
- Flowing, organic appearance
- Natural color blending

SECONDARY: Digital Painting
- Precise details where needed
- Layer blending modes
- Texture overlays
- Professional finishing
```

### Texture Suggestions
- Hair: Flowing watercolor strokes suggesting texture
- Skin: Smooth watercolor layers, no pore detail
- Clothing: Fabric texture suggested, not detailed
- Shoes: Simple texture, not over-detailed

### Color Application
- Warms: Base of warm colors throughout
- Layering: Multiple transparent layers for dimension
- Blending: Soft color transitions
- Accents: Highlights in warm golden tones
- Shadows: Warm shadows (not cool shadows)

### Lighting Consistency
- Consistent light direction throughout story
- Warm primary light (not cool)
- Soft shadows (not harsh)
- Highlights suggesting three-dimensionality
- Atmospheric perspective in backgrounds

## SIZE COMPARISON REFERENCE

When positioning in scenes:

```
Screen Height Placement:
- When standing: Head at 4/5 of image height
- When sitting: Head at 2/3 of image height
- When on ground: Head at 1/2 of image height

Relative to Scene:
- Character typically occupies 30-50% of frame
- Head prominent for expression visibility
- Supporting environment adds context
- Proportions consistent within each story
```

## FILE STANDARDS FOR DELIVERABLES

### Resolution Requirements
- **Print Quality**: 300 DPI minimum
- **File Size**: 3000x3750 pixels (for 10x12.5" print at 300 DPI)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 4:5 (vertical storybook orientation)

### File Format
- **Primary**: PNG (transparency support)
- **Secondary**: High-quality JPG (backup)
- **Working**: PSD or equivalent (for revisions)

### Color Space
- **Working Color Space**: sRGB or wider
- **Final Output**: sRGB for web, CMYK for print
- **Color Management**: Consistent across all pages

### Naming Convention
```
[StoryTheme]_[PageNumber]_[ChildName].png
Example: Adventure_Page01_Emma.png
```

## COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID

❌ **DO NOT**
- Use realistic photography-style rendering
- Include photorealistic skin texture/pores
- Create threatening or scary character
- Use cool-toned gray shadows
- Make anatomically perfect proportions
- Include excessive realistic detail
- Use photo manipulation
- Create character that looks like real child
- Use harsh black outlines (soft watercolor edges)
- Include elements that might frighten young children

✅ **DO**
- Maintain consistently stylized appearance
- Use watercolor or digital painting technique
- Keep warm, optimistic color palette
- Draw with soft, flowing lines
- Exaggerate for emotional expression
- Focus on conveying emotion through facial features
- Use warm neutral shadows and highlights
- Create unique, fictional, illustrated character
- Ensure child-safety and age-appropriateness
- Maintain professional museum-grade quality

## CONTACT & REVISION GUIDELINES

### Revision Request Format
```
Page: [Page Number]
Issue: [Description of what needs change]
Request: [Specific revision needed]
Reference: [Point to specific element]
Preferred: [Suggested fix or style adjustment]
```

### Typical Revision Reasons
- Character consistency (ensure same appearance)
- Expression alignment with story mood
- Color palette adjustment (warming or balancing)
- Detail level (too realistic or too simplified)
- Emotional conveying (expression readability)
- Lighting consistency
- Style consistency with other pages

### Industry Standard Revisions
- Typically: 2-3 revisions included
- Express revisions requested before final delivery
- Style changes may require additional rounds
- Timeline: 3-5 business days per revision round
